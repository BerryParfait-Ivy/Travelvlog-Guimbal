Parish Church
Built in 1730, the San Nicolas de Tolentino Parish Church is one of the oldest structures in Guimbal. It has been home to the Cofradia de la Nuestra Consolacion y Sagrada Correa since 1714, one of the oldest cofradias in the Philippines. Guimbal itself was established earlier than many other Spanish colonial towns in the region.
